Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sky Vanguard mobile application and related services operated by Demented Studios. In this policy, Sky Vanguard is referred to as the "Application" and Demented Studios is referred to as the "Service Provider."

Information Collection and Use

The Application is an Android arcade game. It does not require an account, login, cloud save, public profile, or user-generated content upload feature.

The Application may process information needed to run the app, save local progress, display ads, provide rewarded ad functionality, respond to support requests, and maintain app security and reliability. This may include:

  • Device information such as device type, operating system, app version, approximate region, and Internet Protocol address.
  • App interaction information such as app launches, ad views, rewarded ad interactions, and basic performance or diagnostic data.
  • Local gameplay data stored on your device, such as best score and app settings.
  • Contact information you choose to provide if you email the Service Provider for support or privacy requests.

The Application does not ask you to submit your name, postal address, phone number, precise location, payment information, or account credentials.

Local Save Data

Sky Vanguard may store gameplay and settings data locally on your device. Local save data may include best score, audio preferences, haptic preferences, camera shake preferences, and similar app settings.

This local save data is intended to remain on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the Application, restore from a device backup, or use a feature that explicitly changes how the data is stored.
